A pin is a promise of punishment

A long-range piece aims through an enemy piece at something more valuable behind it. The front piece isn't captured — it's paralyzed. Moving it would expose the treasure.

The core fits in one line. Everything deeper lives in the notes — touch them only if you want more.

hover ✎
Who can pin?
Only sliders — bishop, rook, queen. A knight can never pin anything: it attacks around corners, not through lines.
hover ✎
Absolute vs relative
King behind = absolute: moving off the line is illegal. Queen or rook behind = relative: legal, just usually a terrible idea.
hover ✎
Why it wins games
A pinned piece defends nothing and fears everything. Pile attackers on it — it can't run.
